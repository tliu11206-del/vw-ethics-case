The Cause · Contributing cause

Volkswagen created the conditions that made cheating likely

A huge ambition

Become the world’s largest carmaker, and grow fast in the US with “clean diesel”.

A tight budget

Strict US limits needed costlier technology, which the company would not pay for.

A culture without “no”

Targets came from the top and failure was not accepted, so bending the rules became normal.

The result: a very hard target, a tight budget, and nobody willing to say no.

The Conscience · Pressure

Three pressures pushed the engineers toward the cheat

Pressure 1 · cost

An unaffordable target

Meeting the US standard honestly needed expensive technology, which the finance department refused to fund.

Pressure 2 · the key one

A chain of command

When engineers questioned the device in 2006, they were told to continue and “not get caught”.

Pressure 3 · culture

A code of secrecy

VW’s internal code stressed “secrecy” and “promotion of interest”, so silence felt like loyalty.

Pressure explains behaviour. It does not justify it.

The Conscience · Requirement

Acting ethically meant saying no, at a personal cost

Step 1

Refuse to write the cheating software

Step 2

Tell managers honestly: the engine cannot meet the standard

Step 3

If nobody listens, report the problem to the authorities

The cost

Their projects

Their promotions

Even their jobs

That is the price engineers must be ready to pay when public health is at risk.

Presenter: Peter

The Conscience · Judgement

Ethical only to a very limited extent

In their favour

  • They met the targets on performance and cost
  • They were under real pressure

Against them

  • They were not honest
  • Their product threatened public health
  • They hid the truth for almost ten years

They satisfied the smaller duty by breaking the greater one. Our verdict: unethical.

Conclusion

Engineering ethics is about how we behave when nobody is testing us.

The student fools one teacher. Volkswagen’s engineers fooled regulators around the world, and the public paid the price.
